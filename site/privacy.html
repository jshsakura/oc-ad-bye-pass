OC Ad Bye-Pass

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Short version: OC Ad Bye-Pass collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no telemetry. Nothing about you or your browsing ever leaves your device. The only network requests it makes are to download public filter lists from GitHub.

What we collect

Nothing. The extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. It does not track the pages you visit, does not build a profile, and contains no analytics or advertising code of its own.

What stays on your device

Your settings — which toggles are on, your per-site off list, your custom hiding rules, and a running count of ads blocked — are stored locally by your browser. If your browser has extension sync enabled, your browser (not us) may sync those settings across your own devices. We never receive them. You can reset the counter or clear everything at any time from the settings page.

Network requests the extension makes

  • Filter lists. It periodically downloads a public JSON rule file from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) so blocking stays current. This is an ordinary file download — a plain GET — and carries no information about you.
  • Version check. It reads a small public file on GitHub to see whether a newer build exists. Again, a file download; nothing about you is sent.

That is the complete list. The extension talks to no other server, and it contacts no advertising or analytics service.

Permissions, and why each is needed

  • storage — save your settings and block count on your device.
  • activeTab — read the current tab's address when you click the icon, so it can show the on/off switch for that site. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • declarativeNetRequest — block ad and tracker requests by rule. Your browser applies the rules; the extension does not read or receive your network traffic.
  • scripting — run the on-page component that removes ad slots mixed into a page.
  • Site access — fetch filter lists from GitHub, and let the on-page component hide ad slots on the sites where you keep the extension enabled.

Third parties

None. GitHub hosts the public filter-list files the extension downloads, so a request to GitHub is subject to GitHub's own privacy practices — but that is a file download, not a sharing of your data. No data is sent to us or to anyone else.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone regardless of age.

Open source

OC Ad Bye-Pass is free and open source under the GPLv3. You can read exactly what it does — every line — at github.com/jshsakura/oc-ad-bye-pass.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Material changes will be noted in the project's releases.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue or a discussion at the project's GitHub.
